# Setting: 19th-century Far East treaty ports

The game is set among the 19th-century treaty ports (Hong Kong, Shanghai, Nagasaki, Singapore, Manila), matching its Taipan! inspiration and the existing ports, goods (including opium), ships and dollar currency. The picul is the single unit for both goods and Hold capacity, simplified for play: one unit the Captain can reason about, glossed once in the help screen as about 60 kg.

## Considered Options

- **13th-century Song/Yuan maritime routes**: rejected because every port, good and unit (jin, dan, liao, copper cash) would change, with no gain for the planned features.
- **16th–17th-century Age of Sail** (Macau, Malacca, Batavia, Ming sea ban): attractive for smuggling and discovery, but still a full rename of ports and goods.
